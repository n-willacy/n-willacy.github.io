The Whip

A party whip has 15 moves to get around the Capitol before the roll call. Three members are wavering (dashed circles): visit them to secure their votes. One member is loyal (blue): visiting them changes nothing, because their vote was never in doubt. The real goal: secure all three wavering votes, so 3 of the 4 members are locked in, before time runs out.

Q-learning agent

The agent keeps a table with one row per state: its square plus which wavering members are secured. That is states × 4 moves. Settings: explore 20% of the time (ε = 0.2), learning rate 0.2, discount 0.95, random seed 42.

Learning curve

Average reward per game (left axis) Share of games that secured all 3 votes (right axis)

Each point summarizes 50 training games. Training games include the 20% random exploring, and the best route leaves only 2 spare moves, so a few random steps often cost the whole game. That is why the curve sits well below what "Watch it play" shows.
